# Publishing on Amazon KDP (Rayhan's steps)

The three books are finished and pass every check in `dist/*-qc.json`. Only you can do the steps below,
because KDP needs your own identity, bank account and tax details.

1. Sign up at https://kdp.amazon.com with your own Amazon account. Fill in **Your Account**: legal name,
   Edmonton address, Canadian bank account (paid by EFT in CAD) and the **tax interview** (W-8BEN as a
   Canadian individual; claim the Canada-US treaty so US withholding drops below the default 30 %; the
   interview shows the rate it applies).
2. Bookshelf > **+ Create** > **Paperback**. For each book, copy the fields from `LISTINGS.md`:
   - Details: title, subtitle, author (pen name), description, "I own the copyright", 7 keywords, 3 categories.
     Low-content: unticked. Large print: unticked.
   - Content: "Get a free KDP ISBN". Ink and paper: black and white on white. Trim: 8.5 x 11 in.
     Bleed: **No bleed**. Cover finish: matte. Upload `dist/<book>-interior.pdf` and, under "Upload a cover
     you already have", `dist/<book>-cover.pdf`.
   - **AI-generated content: Yes.** Text: "the puzzles and layout were generated by software". Images: same.
   - Launch the **Previewer**, check a few pages, then Approve.
   - Pricing: Amazon.com USD 9.99; set Amazon.ca to CAD 13.99 by hand (not the auto-converted price),
     leave Expanded Distribution off. Click **Publish your paperback book**.
3. KDP allows 2 new paperbacks per week for a new account: publish Calcudoku and Nonogram this week,
   Kakuro next week. Review takes up to 72 hours.

Optional: order one author copy of each (printing cost plus shipping, about USD 3 + shipping) to check
the print before advertising. This costs money, so it is your call.

## Second channel (optional, needs a Gumroad or Payhip account in your name)

Sign up at https://gumroad.com (or https://payhip.com), create a digital product per book with the text
in `LISTINGS.md` > Gumroad / Payhip, and upload the matching `dist/*-interior.pdf`. No upfront cost.

## What to expect

| | |
|---|---|
| Royalty per paperback | USD 3.06 on Amazon.com, CAD 4.74 on Amazon.ca |
| Deadline | none; this is a store listing, not a contest |
| Realistic sales | 0 to 5 copies per book per month without ads; many puzzle books sell nothing |
| Expected payout | about USD 0 to 45 per month for all three; most likely under USD 10 |

KDP pays monthly, about 60 days after the end of the month of sale. Competition checked on Amazon.com on 2026-11-21:
"large print kenken" 184 results (low), "calcudoku puzzle book" 3,000, "nonogram puzzle book" 2,000,
"large print kakuro" 4,000, against 50,000 for "large print sudoku". Calcudoku is the best bet.
